Where your data goes

{% if hosted %}Records and reviews are stored on the hosting server. Use sample data in this test deployment. The site owner and hosting operator can access stored files.{% else %}Records and reviews are stored on this computer. A person with access to the workspace files can read them.{% endif %}

Database files are not encrypted by the application. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. This release does not provide end-to-end encryption or separate private workspaces.

No advertising, analytics or tracking scripts are included. Activity logs are trimmed to the last 30 days or 10,000 events as the app is used. Downloaded files remain under your control outside the app.

MedSafe's optional terminology lookup sends medicine codes to NLM, without patient names or history. It requires an internet connection. Imported records remain until the owner removes the workspace; automatic patient-data deletion is not yet available.
