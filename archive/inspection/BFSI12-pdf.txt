Problem Statement 2: Intelligent Life Insurance Needs Finder & Digital Advisory 
Platform 
Problem Statement 
Navigating life insurance purchases is often overwhelming for retail banking 
customers due to complex financial jargon, manual coverage estimation, and a lack of 
personalized guidance. This leads to high drop -off rates and low digital sales 
conversion for insurance providers. BFSI institutions need an end-to-end digital advice 
platform that replaces static sales funnels with an intelligent needs -assessment 
engine—one that calculates precise coverage requirements, matches users to tailored 
life insurance products, and provides interactive advisory features to complete the 
purchase journey seamlessly. 
Student Goal 
Develop an interactive customer -facing web or mobile platform that captures user 
financial parameters, runs automated algorithms to calculate their exact life  
insurance needs (such as Human Life Value or Net Needs Approach), recommends 
appropriate insurance products based on profile matching, and offers integrated 
digital advice capabilities (such as policy customization, scenario modeling, and 
specialist consultation booking). 
Expected Outcome 
Financial Needs Calculation Engine: A functional backend algorithm that accurately 
evaluates user inputs (Age, Annual Income, Dependents, Outstanding Liabilities, 
Future Goals) to recommend an optimal life cover sum. 
Algorithmic Recommendation Engine : A dynamic decision engine that maps  
calculated coverage gaps, budget constraints, and risk profiles to specific insurance 
product types (e.g., Term Life, Whole Life, Endowment, ULIPs). 
Interactive Frontend Journey:  A responsive digital interface (Web, App, or Streamlit 
application) leading users through a seamless four-stage experience: 
1. Assessment Questionnaire / Profile Inputs 
2. Calculated Coverage Recommendation & Visual Breakdown 
3. Product Selection & Interactive Benefit Tweaking (slider adjustments for 
premiums/tenure) 
4. Digital Advisory Module (a simulated video/chat consultation scheduler with a 
financial advisor)
